🐂 Welcome to FitBull

Answer a few questions (about 10 minutes) and FitBull builds your training and food plan, sets up this Sheet, schedules your morning email and publishes your phone app.

Your data stays in this Sheet, in your own Google account. Nobody else – including whoever shared FitBull with you – can see it.

FitBull gives general fitness coaching, not medical advice.

Your free AI key

FitBull's coach, food photos and meal ideas use Google's Gemini AI – with your own free key.

  1. Open aistudio.google.com/apikey (same Google account).
  2. Click Create API key and copy it with the Copy button (a long mix of letters and numbers – the exact start doesn't matter).
  3. Paste it here and tap Check.

Tip: use the copy icon next to the key on that page. You can also skip this and add it later (FitBull ▸ Change my goals or plan) – FitBull still works, just without the AI features.
